The CrystaLLM-π preprint is on arXiv! We inject continuous properties straight into the transformer's attention, so the model keeps what it learned in pre-training while being steered towards a target. Example applications in the paper include recovering structures from X-ray diffraction patterns, and discover new photovoltaic candidates that we then checked with DFT.
